ZOCO Privacy Policy

Effective 10 July 2026 · Applies to the ZOCO website, web app and Android app.

ZOCO helps you understand what's in packaged food. We collect the minimum needed to run the service, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, managed by Google Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google (web only), Google shares your name, email and profile photo with us.
  • Usage tied to your account: a count of how many product scans you have used, and your membership/subscription status. We do not keep a list of which products you scanned.
  • Payments: subscriptions are processed by Razorpay. Your card/UPI details go directly to Razorpay — we never receive or store them. We store only your subscription id and status.
  • Corrections you submit: if you report a product-data error, we store the report (product barcode, the correction text) for review.
  • Anonymous analytics: basic usage events (e.g. "a scan happened") without personal identifiers, used to improve the app.

What stays on your device

  • Camera: barcode scanning happens entirely on your device. Camera images are never uploaded or stored — only the decoded barcode number is sent to look up the product.
  • Family profile: your diet, allergen, fasting and language preferences are stored only in your browser/app storage, never on our servers.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and account management (Firebase privacy).
  • Razorpay — payment processing (Razorpay privacy).
  • Open Food Facts — the public product database we query. Only the barcode or your search text is sent; not your identity.
  • Vercel — hosting; standard server logs (IP address, request path) are kept briefly for security and abuse prevention.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app:

  • Open the app → tap the scans badge in the header (your account page) → Delete account → confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes your account and subscription record, and cancels any active subscription so you are not charged again. It cannot be undone. Residual anti-abuse counters (your scan count, no longer linked to any account) are purged automatically within 30 days. If you cannot access the app, email us from your registered address and we will delete the account for you within 7 days.

Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists and removed on deletion. Correction reports may be kept after review in anonymised form (the product fix itself, not your identity). Anonymous analytics contain no personal data.

Children

ZOCO is a general-audience food-information app and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The family profile (e.g. "kids" guidance) is stored only on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the effective date above.
